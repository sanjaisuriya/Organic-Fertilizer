Transition Protocol

3-Step Path from Chemical Dependency to Living Soil

Restoring farmland fertility is straightforward when guided by biological principles. Here is our field-proven schedule for progressive growers.

Step 01 • Basal Prep
Soil Detox & Microbial Revival

Apply 3–5 tons/acre of 180-Day Cured Cow Dung Manure during primary deep tilling. Buffers residual synthetic chemical salts, loosens compacted clay hardpans, and inoculates billions of beneficial aerobic soil bacteria.

Apply 15–20 days prior to sowing
Step 02 • Root Armor
Feeder Root Armor & Nutrition

Band-apply Enriched Vermicompost combined with Pure Neem Cake Fertilizer directly into seed furrows or sapling root zones. Forms an active natural defense against root grubs and nematodes while releasing organic N-P-K.

Apply at seed sowing / transplanting
Step 03 • Yield Boost
Flowering Support & Fruit Weight

Top-dress with Aerobic Compost Manure and Bio-Organic Potash before flowering stage. Maximizes brix sugar sweetness, enhances grain-filling density, protects against summer heat stress, and locks in peak harvest gains.

Apply during active vegetative surge
