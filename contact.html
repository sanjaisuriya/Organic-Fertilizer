How Our Direct Farmgate Supply Works

From your initial consultation to doorstep unloading, we ensure verified organic quality, exact weighbridge tonnage, and dependable transit timelines.

Step 01
Submit Crop Requirements

All bulk organic manure dispatches are covered by official batch laboratory certificates guaranteeing C:N ratio, moisture tolerance (≤ 25%), weed-free sterilization, and zero chemical adulteration. Deliveries are scheduled via GPS-tracked commercial tipper trucks and pan-India freight with weighbridge slips provided at loading and dispatch.
