Our Process

From Natural Ingredients to Nutrient-Rich Manure.

Scientific Organic Engineering

The 5 Stages of TerraPure Biological Curing

From ethical raw material sourcing to thermophilic windrow turning and strict quality certification, every batch undergoes a 180-day biological maturation protocol.

01
Ethical Raw Sourcing
Indigenous Biomass

We partner with over 4,500 local dairy cooperatives and agricultural farms to collect pure, unadulterated indigenous cattle dung, cold-pressed botanical seed cakes, and agricultural residues.

02
Thermophilic Windrow Curing
65°C Heat Pasteurization

Biomass is arranged in 100-meter aerobic windrows and turned regularly with specialized machinery. Internal core temperatures naturally rise to 65°C–70°C, destroying weed seeds and pathogens.

03
Vermiculture Bio-Enrichment
Earthworm Castings & Auxins

Pre-cured organic material is introduced into shaded vermicomposting beds populated with millions of Eisenia fetida earthworms, transforming carbon into enzymatic humic black gold.

04
Rotary Sifting & Screening
Uniform Crumb Aggregation

Cured manure passes through multi-tier rotary drum trommels to filter out stones, hard debris, and uncomposted twigs, producing a uniform, free-flowing, granular texture.

05
Lab Assay & Certification
14-Parameter Quality Audit

Every production batch is tested in our accredited agronomy laboratory for C:N ratio (15:1–20:1), moisture tolerance (≤ 25%), and zero synthetic chemicals or heavy metals.

06
Moisture-Lock Packaging
Pan-India Freight Logistics

Formulations are packed in breathable UV-stabilized woven HDPE bags (25kg, 50kg, and 1-ton jumbo bags) or dispatched in GPS-tracked bulk tippers directly to farm gates.
