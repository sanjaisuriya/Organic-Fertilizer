Who We Are

Dedicated Stewards of Regenerative Soil Health

Founded over 25 years ago in the agricultural heartland of Tamil Nadu, TerraPure Organics & Bio-Manure began with a single passionate goal: to heal depleted, salt-damaged soil by replacing harmful synthetic chemicals with biological, pathogen-free organic nutrition.

Today, we are one of South India's largest certified suppliers of bulk organic fertilizers, matured indigenous cow dung manures, vermicompost, and neem cake conditioners. We partner with over 4,500 local dairy keepers and agricultural cooperatives to aggregate pure raw biomass, which we transform into stabilized, lab-tested plant food through scientific windrow thermophilic composting.

Our state-of-the-art centralized composting facility handles over 50,000 metric tons annually, operating on a closed-loop biological recovery model. By directly compensating pastoral livestock owners for raw manure, we provide steady supplementary income to agrarian communities while ensuring 100% purity, zero heavy metal contamination, and guaranteed weed-free biological amendments for sustainable farming nationwide.

Scientific Rigor

Our 6-Stage Quality Assurance Protocol

Unlike raw untreated dung that harbors root maggots, weed seeds, and Salmonella, every batch of TerraPure manure undergoes an uncompromising 6-stage biological curing standard.

Stage 01
Pure Biomass Sourcing

Direct collection of unadulterated cow dung and raw organic biomass from pasture-grazed cattle, rigorously inspected to be free from synthetic hormones, chemical residues, plastic debris, and stones.

Stage 02
Thermophilic Windrow Curing

Maintained within a controlled thermophilic window of 60°C–65°C for at least 21 days with regular mechanical turning. Natural biological heat pasteurizes weed seeds, fungal spores, insect larvae, and pathogens.

Stage 03
Worm Digestion (Vermicompost)

After the thermophilic stage, the cured compost is allowed to cool down to ambient temperatures before introducing millions of active Eisenia fetida earthworms, which enrich the organic matter with vital plant auxins, enzymes, and bio-available humic acids.

Stage 04
Rotary Sifting & Screening

High-efficiency rotary trommel screens and vibratory multi-mesh sieves grade and refine the compost into a uniform, tea-ground texture with zero lumps, uncomposted woody debris, or gravel.

Stage 05
Laboratory Lot Certification

Comprehensive analytical spectrometry certifies NPK macronutrients, organic carbon, moisture content (target 18–22%), and C:N ratio (<20:1), with laboratory test reports verifying heavy metals are well within permissible statutory limits.

Stage 06
Moisture-Lock Packing

Sealed in heavy-duty, UV-stabilized, multi-layer woven polypropylene bags designed for superior moisture protection, preserving live beneficial microflora and guaranteed product quality throughout transit and extended on-farm storage.
